Planning reliability: planner and frozen A2 navigator

These are bounded fresh-seed starts on familiar maps. Both branches restore the same settled spawn, game RNG, gems and score before playing. They stop at the first fall or 600 decisions; no rescue or teleport occurs during play. This is a short-attempt comparison, not a full-round or unseen-map benchmark.

Choose Recording, then Focus trajectory and scrub Time to see the entire measured path and game score. Cyan is native motion. Coral joins short predictions that are reanchored as the controller observes the game; it is not one long open-loop forecast. Gaps explicitly mean prediction unavailable for external events. The predictor is independent analytical mechanics; no policy or dynamics model was trained here.
